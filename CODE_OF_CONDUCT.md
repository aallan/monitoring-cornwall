# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make taking part in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment includes:

- using welcoming and inclusive language
- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- focusing on what is best for the community and the project

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks
- publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission
- any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel associated with the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainer at alasdair@babilim.co.uk. Every report will be reviewed and investigated, and will receive a response appropriate to the circumstances.
