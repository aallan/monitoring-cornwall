About

A map of Cornwall's rivers, streams and railways, drawn from OpenStreetMap. Rivers end at their tidal limit rather than running down the estuaries to the sea. The railways are the passenger and freight network; heritage lines aren't shown.

The code is open source, under the MIT licence, at github.com/aallan/monitoring-cornwall. Corrections to rivers or railways are best made on OpenStreetMap itself, and reach this map the next time it's rebuilt.
