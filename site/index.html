About

A map of Cornwall's rivers, streams, railways and major roads, drawn from OpenStreetMap, with the Environment Agency's flood warnings and alerts, live readings from its river level, tidal and rain gauges, and South West Water's storm overflow monitors. Each river ends where it meets the dimmed water outside the county, rather than running down the middle of the estuary to the sea. The railways are the passenger and freight network; heritage lines aren't shown. The roads are the A30, A38 and A39.

The code is open source, under the MIT licence, at github.com/aallan/monitoring-cornwall. Corrections to rivers or railways are best made on OpenStreetMap itself, and reach this map the next time it's rebuilt.
